# ISRG Unit Economics — Integrated-System Cost Stack (PIL-1)

## Executive Summary

ISRG's integrated-system economics: da Vinci ASP ~$1.60M FY2025 (up from ~$1.50M FY2024; ~$1.66M H1-2026) plus ~$1,826 of instruments-and-accessories (I&A) revenue per procedure FY2025 (~$1,852 Q2-2026) and ~$141.6K service per installed system-year. Recurring revenue is 84% of FY2025 total (85% Q2-2026) on an 11,106-system installed base. Product gross margin FY2023→FY2025: 65.7% → 67.2% → 66.3% (total GM 66.0% FY2025); the FY2025 dip is tariff/depreciation/da Vinci 5 cost-driven, and Q2-2026's 67.9% rebound embeds $27.5M one-off IEEPA tariff refunds. Cost stack: product COGS $2,866.1M vs service $556.3M — 83.7%/16.3% of total COGS. No filing-level BOM disclosure: product COGS intensity of 33.7% is the ceiling proxy for the PIL-1a BOM-share test. All T-001 baselines verified PASS.

## Core Analysis

### 1. Per-system economics (the motion-layer unit)

Disclosed pricing bands [FACT]: the da Vinci system sells for $0.7M–$3.1M (inclusive of one year of service), service contracts renew at $95K–$225K per system-year, and I&A earn $900–$3,700 per procedure (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page77).

Blended realizations [FACT]: da Vinci ASP (excluding operating-lease placements, Ion, and trade-ins) was ~$1.50M FY2024 and ~$1.60M FY2025 (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page78); H1-2026 ASP ~$1.66M vs ~$1.55M H1-2025, driven by da Vinci 5 mix (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page40). This is the full trajectory claimed in T-001: $1.50M → $1.60M → $1.66M.

Per-procedure economics [DEDUCTED]: I&A revenue ÷ procedures (da Vinci + Ion) = $6,018.9M ÷ (3,153.0K + 144.1K) = **$1,826** FY2025; Q2-2026: $1,734.9M ÷ (889.0K + 47.9K) = **$1,852** — consistent with the ~$1.83K anchor (inputs: https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page69, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page77, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page32, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page38). Procedure volume grew 18% FY2025 (3,153,000 da Vinci; 144,100 Ion) with utilization +3% (procedures per system per year) (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page69).

Service per system-year [DEDUCTED]: $1,572.1M service revenue ÷ 11,106 installed = **$141.6K** per system-year FY2025, mid-range of the $95K–$225K contract band (inputs: https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page69, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page77). Q2-2026 installed base 11,710 (+12% YoY) (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page32).

Attachment economics [FACT]: recurring revenue (I&A + service + operating lease) = $8,465.3M, 84% of FY2025 revenue; 85% in Q2-2026 ($2,469.1M) (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page78, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page40).

### 2. Product vs service cost structure

FY2025: product revenue $8,492.6M with product gross profit $5,626.5M; service revenue $1,572.1M with service gross profit $1,015.8M [FACT] (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page77, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page79).

| Line (FY2025, $M) | Revenue | Gross profit | COGS [DEDUCTED] | GM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product | 8,492.6 | 5,626.5 | 2,866.1 | 66.3% |
| Service | 1,572.1 | 1,015.8 | 556.3 | 64.6% |
| Total | 10,064.7 | 6,642.3 | 3,422.4 | 66.0% |

Product COGS is 83.7% of total COGS [DEDUCTED]. The derived COGS column reconciles exactly to XBRL CostOfGoodsAndServicesSold of $3,422.4M FY2025, $2,717.9M FY2024, $2,394.6M FY2023 (is_primary, 10-K authority) — and revenue reconciles to XBRL RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax ($10,064.7M / $8,352.1M / $7,124.1M).

Product GM drivers [FACT]: FY2025 decline from 67.2% to 66.3% was caused by new tariffs, incremental fixed overhead/depreciation from expanded manufacturing capacity, and higher da Vinci 5 costs, partly offset by lower excess-and-obsolete inventory charges; product COGS included $120.7M share-based compensation and $9.0M intangible amortization (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page79). Q2-2026 product GM 67.9% was boosted by $27.5M of IEEPA tariff refunds credited to product COGS — a one-off (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page41).

Service GM drivers [FACT]: FY2025 service GM fell 69.0% → 64.6% (da Vinci 5 service costs, unfavorable repair mix, fixed costs, tariffs); Q2-2026 recovered to 67.2% (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page79, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page41). Product-vs-service GM gap: service led product by 4.1pp in FY2023, narrowed to 1.8pp in FY2024, and inverted to product leading by 1.7pp in FY2025 [DEDUCTED].

### 4. Cost-stack decomposition and the PIL-1a BOM-share proxy

[FACT] Within product COGS, ISRG quantifies only share-based compensation ($120.7M; 4.2% of product COGS [DEDUCTED]) and intangible amortization ($9.0M) (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page79). Tariff expense, depreciation (from expanded manufacturing capacity), and da Vinci 5 costs are described qualitatively but not dollarized within product COGS (https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec166/page79, https://agentii.ai/v/ISRG/sec172/page41).

[VIEW] Because no filing-level materials/BOM disclosure exists, the strongest available proxy for the PIL-1a actuation-plus-sensing BOM-share test is product COGS intensity — 33.7% of product revenue FY2025 [DEDUCTED from claims above]. This is a ceiling: actual direct-materials BOM share of the integrated system's cost is below one-third of product revenue, and the falsifier comparison should not be made directly against it without a Deviation Register note.

[VIEW] The razor/razor-blade structure (recurring 84–85%, ~$1.83K per procedure, utilization +3%) means the motion-layer profit pool is dominated by per-procedure consumables and service rather than the ~$1.6M hardware transaction — relevant to PIL-1's "motion-layer profit evidence" and PIL-3's per-unit denominator.
